## [Huawei Introduces 'Tao's Law' for Semiconductor Evolution](https://www.peopleapp.com/column/30052220655-500007509895) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Huawei has introduced 'Tao's Law' at the 2026 International Circuit and Systems Seminar in Shanghai. This new principle proposes replacing geometric scaling with time scaling in semiconductor development. 'Tao's Law' could potentially overcome the limitations of Moore's Law by enabling more efficient chip designs. This approach may lead to significant advancements in semiconductor technology and industry collaboration. Huawei has already designed and mass-produced 381 chips based on this principle over the past six years. The company plans to release new Kirin chips using logic folding technology this fall.

telegram · zaihuapd · May 25, 01:35

**Background**: Moore's Law, which predicts the doubling of transistors on a microchip approximately every two years, is reaching its physical limits. Huawei's 'Tao's Law' introduces time scaling as an alternative, focusing on reducing time constants for multi-level optimization. This approach aims to achieve transistor densities comparable to future 1.4 nm processes by 2031.

## [Constraint Decay in LLM Agents: Challenges in Backend Code Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06445) ⭐️ 7.0/10

The article introduces 'constraint decay', a phenomenon where LLM-based coding agents show decreased performance when strict architectural rules are applied. This issue affects their reliability in backend code generation. Understanding constraint decay is crucial as it highlights a significant limitation of LLMs in production-grade backend development. This could impact developers relying on AI for complex coding tasks. The study found that LLM agents perform well under baseline conditions but struggle with explicit architectural constraints, losing significant assertion pass rates. The study did not fully test frontier models due to cost constraints.

hackernews · wek · May 24, 12:55 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48256912)

**Background**: LLM-based coding agents are AI systems that autonomously generate code, often used to speed up development processes. 'Constraint decay' refers to the degradation of performance when these agents are required to adhere to specific coding standards or architectural rules. This issue is particularly relevant in backend development, where strict adherence to architectural guidelines is crucial for system reliability.
